Reading Hockey Club archive

Every men's National League, cup, European and indoor match we've been able to trace, with scorers, lineups where they survive, league tables and player records.

Finding your way around

  • Matches: every game by season. Click a match to see the scorers and, where known, the lineup. Filter by season, team, type or competition, or search for an opponent or player.
  • Stats: top scorers and appearances, or season-by-season records. Switch between outdoor and indoor.
  • Players: a page for every player, with their goals, appearances and matches.
  • Opponents: Reading's record against every club. Click a club for its own page.
  • Tables: final league tables, cup runs, European campaigns and indoor seasons.

Dates, scores and scorers come from newspaper archives, club records and England Hockey. Gaps are marked: "Score not known", "Date unknown", or "Unknown" for a goal whose scorer couldn't be traced.

Add match

Score is always from Reading's side. Lineups and scorers are optional; leave the lineup off if no team sheet survives.

Fixture

Cup games: put the cup in Competition and the round in Round (leave End-of-season competition blank). For anything after the league (play-offs, a championship weekend, a super cup), fill in the end-of-season competition and/or the round. Leave the competition blank and it's called "Play-offs". These matches appear in the bracket under that season's league table. League stage is only needed for split seasons.

Competition types

Each competition is sorted from its name: "Indoor", "Super 6s" = indoor (European if it also says "Euro"); "Cup", "Trophy" = domestic cup; "Euro", "European", "Cup Winners" = European cup; anything else = league. Change any that are wrong here.

Data

Where your data is saved, backups, and tidying up names.

Saving

To publish, put reading_hc_data.json next to this page on GitHub Pages. The hosted site then opens read-only with that data.

Backup

One HTML file with today's data built in. People just double-click it: nothing to connect, and it opens view-only so they can't change anything. Send a new copy when the data changes.

Gaps report

An Excel workbook of everything still to find, across leagues, cups, Europe and indoor: missing league games (outdoor and indoor), games with scorers missing or incomplete, venue clashes or duplicates, and games with no date. Built from the data as it is now.

Import matches from a spreadsheet

Copy rows from Excel and paste them here. With the match template, each column is read exactly as it sits in the template workbook (copying the header row too is fine; it's skipped). Free-form reads older sheets laid out as season, opponent, H/A, score, result, competition, then the line up. A row that matches a match already in the archive (same team, season, opponent, venue and score) fills in what's missing; anything else is added as a new match.

Rename or merge

Fixes a name across all matches and league tables, or only within one team. Renaming a player to someone who already exists merges the two records.
